INITIATIVE 01 Quality Risk Management · Regulatory

Bringing Remdesivir API to Egypt — under pandemic pressure

During COVID-19, import limits left Egypt critically short of Remdesivir for severe cases. EVA Pharma decided to manufacture the API domestically — a first for the country. I took the initiative to join the project team as the responsible QA member, charged with starting production fast without compromising quality or safety.

What I did

  • Studied the preparation process and attended trial-batch manufacturing first-hand to clarify quality requirements as inputs for the risk assessment.
  • As the QA member owning QRM and validation, I quantified the risks of a hazardous new API using objective risk-assessment tools — separating acceptable risks from those that demanded additional controls.
  • Aligned internal stakeholders (planning, production, engineering) who first resisted the extra controls — showing objectively how they prevent deviations and cross-contamination. QA became a proactive partner, not the brake on production.
  • Handled external regulators (authorities & WHO/MoH representatives) through repeated rounds of review — presenting the risk assessments and added precautions, resolving every comment until the manufacturing licence was granted.
What I'm proudest of is the human scale of this: an initiative that drove global impact. Knowing I helped deliver a critically needed treatment to patients during a national shortage — getting it right when lives depended on the batch behind it — is a feeling that stays with me.

INITIATIVE 02 Validation · Process Improvement

Cutting autoclave validation downtime by 50%

As Senior QA Specialist responsible for validation, I took it on myself to reduce validation downtime while maintaining — or improving — compliance, rather than accept the status quo of repeating low-value cycles.

What I did

  • Studied the guidelines in depth — ICH, EudraLex and the manufacturer's manuals.
  • Ran a risk-based assessment of sterilisation loads, using data from previous cycles to identify which loads genuinely represented the worst cases.
  • Validated the approach with experts — the autoclave manufacturer's (Fedegari) technical team and, via a webinar speaker, an engineering manager at Abbott (USA) I still stay in contact with.
  • Redesigned the validation matrix around true worst cases instead of repeating low-risk scenarios; secured my manager's approval, then sold the timeline and savings to other departments.
  • Updated the SOPs and validation protocols and trained operators and QA staff so everyone understood the rationale.
My biggest takeaway: how to influence without authority — using data to convince sceptical stakeholders and lead improvement that balances quality with efficiency.

Freezer Temperature Gap Checker

A lightweight, browser-based QA tool that turns the slow, error-prone daily cold-chain log review at a plasma collection site into a few seconds.
